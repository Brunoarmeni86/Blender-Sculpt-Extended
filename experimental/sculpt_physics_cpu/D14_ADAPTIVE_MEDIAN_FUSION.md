# Adaptive Median Fusion — shared material-interface principle
The tool-specific displacement remains the source of the proposed deposit. This separate stage fits a robust local median of trial-height residuals in tangent-detrended coordinates, then exchanges material across oriented incident mesh edges subject to donor/receiver budgets. Existing relief gates fusion; flat or planar sloping first contact retains the raw tool profile. Unlike the prior D13/D14 bulb fusion, a donor may finish with negative axial displacement (controlled mobilisation of the previous live surface), funded by a local new-deposit-dependent allowance. Net area-weighted axial displacement is conserved before the native geometry guard; exact enclosed volume and post-write conservation are not claimed. Strict capacity and balance checks may reject a GPU exchange and retry the CPU oracle. No generic Smooth pass. No motion in the absence of deposition.

V1 opt-in BLENDER_SCULPT_D14_MEDIAN_FUSION=1 supersedes the older fusion stage only for positive dedicated D12 Clay-family deposition, including D14 ClayBulb if separately enabled. Existing individual tip and deposit laws remain intact. ClayStrips/Build use their existing D12 law. Host writable envelope, thin-contact selection if enabled, first-touch cap, native guard and Undo are reused. It deliberately works only on vertices with nonzero raw deposition and sufficient headroom. Prior live geometry is a proxy for overlap, not explicit identification of material deposited in earlier strokes; an original ridge can also be mobilised. This is a one-ring prototype, resolution-dependent and not a full robust surface fit or physical volume solver. Default mobility .65, old material cap .025R per dab, relaxation .65. These are calibration choices. GPU currently accelerates only the existing paired exchange, not median or geometry construction. May fall back to CPU due to strict double-precision validation. Logs [D14 Median Fusion], links, old_donors, old_mobilized, balance are proposal diagnostics, not accepted native motion metrics. No new attributes or persistent per-stroke provenance. Other physical brush actions are not wired; extend only with action-specific material laws/permissions (no additive fusion on Scrape or carving).

Tests: test_d14_adaptive_median_fusion checks prior-material movement, area-weighted balance, native support, tilted-plane preservation, no spontaneous motion, headroom and transactional invalid input. Not compiled or run at publication. Future: actual hit-face and material provenance, physical-radius neighbourhood, after-write conservation diagnostics, GPU median/transport, extension to eligible nondeposit actions and volumetric solvers. Avoid universal smoothing.
